ONION: A Multi-Layered Framework for Participatory ER Design

Viktoriia Makovska, George Fletcher, Julia Stoyanovich

How can people recognize their own needs in a formal data model?

The questionA visual guideWhat the work contributesRead the source

The question

Why this work

ONION organizes participatory entity–relationship design into five stages. It carries open-ended stakeholder accounts into a structured model, with opportunities to check whether technical abstraction has lost something important.

The approach

A visual guide

What the work contributes

Workshops with Donor UA and UCU’s Volunteers Lab surfaced overlooked relationships and “invisible” entities. Participants could trace contributions into the resulting models and described changes in their understanding of other roles.
