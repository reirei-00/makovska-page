Seasoning Data Modeling Education with GARLIC: A Participatory Co-Design Framework

Viktoriia Makovska, Ihor Michurin, Mariia Tokhtamysh, George Fletcher, Julia Stoyanovich

How can role-play teach students to represent different needs in a data model?

The questionA visual guideWhat the work contributesRead the source

The question

Why this work

GARLIC turns participatory entity–relationship modeling into a teachable workshop. A shared scenario and role cards help participants articulate conflicting needs before translating them into entities, relationships, and constraints.

The approach

A visual guide

What the work contributes

Two initial 90-minute pilots, with five participants each, revealed a tendency to jump into technical solutions before representing stakeholder voices. These observations led to clearer advocacy roles, simpler starting scenarios, and more explicit checks for missing perspectives.
