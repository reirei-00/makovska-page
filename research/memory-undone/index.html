Memory Undone: Between Knowing and Not Knowing in Data Systems

Viktoriia Makovska, George Fletcher, Julia Stoyanovich, Tetiana Zakharchenko

What disappears when a data system “forgets”—and who decides?

The questionA visual guideWhat the work contributesRead the source

The question

Why this work

This position paper treats forgetting as both a technical intervention and a social practice. Removing a file, changing a trained model, and never collecting a perspective can all make information unavailable, but through different mechanisms.

The approach

A visual guide

What the work contributes

The paper offers a vocabulary for separating these mechanisms and argues for accountability around what is retained or excluded. Evaluating forgetting therefore involves rights, power, and representation alongside technical utility.
