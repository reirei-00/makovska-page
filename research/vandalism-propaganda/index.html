Vandalism or Propaganda? Enhancing Vandalism Detection in Ukrainian and Russian Wikipedia through Knowledge Manipulation Filtering

Viktoriia Makovska, Mykola Trokhymovych, Diego Saez-Trumper

Can a politically controlled Wikipedia fork reveal subtler forms of manipulation?

The questionA visual guideWhat the work contributesRead the source

The question

Why this work

Obvious vandalism is not the only way to distort an encyclopedia. This symposium paper explores a Russian government-backed Wikipedia fork as a source of signals about narrative changes in Russian and Ukrainian Wikipedia edits.

The approach

A visual guide

What the work contributes

The exploratory analysis found overlapping edits that were accepted in the fork but frequently reverted in official Wikipedia. This supports investigating fork-derived signals as an addition to vandalism detection.
