Graph-Based Detection of Disinformation Narrative Diffusion between Russian and Ukrainian Telegram Channels

Yuliia Vistak, Viktoriia Makovska, Vera Schmitt, Veronika Solopova

How do you follow a narrative when it is paraphrased and reposted?

The questionA visual guideWhat the work contributesRead the source

The question

Why this work

Individual messages reveal only part of a disinformation campaign. This paper combines narrative labels with graph structure to examine how related claims circulate between Russian and Ukrainian Telegram channels.

The approach

A visual guide

What the work contributes

Semantic links expose repost-like connections that native forwarding metadata misses. The two graph layers give different views of channel connectivity, revealing why tracking explicit forwards alone can understate narrative circulation.

Sections 3–5 and Limitations

Evidence & limits

Weak labels can be wrong and narrative categories change over time. A semantic connection suggests related content; it does not establish who copied whom or prove coordination. The framework supports research and human review.
